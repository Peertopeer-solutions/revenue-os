- 2026-10-03 · **A proof step's time limit is kept by its commands**: every command a step starts through the proof's helpers (scripts/proof/time-limit.ts) is stopped when the step's time is up, since a blocking command holds off any timer; a step that ran past its time is failed whatever it returned; and a run's steps together get 100 minutes, so its report is written before proof.yml's 120-minute limit.
