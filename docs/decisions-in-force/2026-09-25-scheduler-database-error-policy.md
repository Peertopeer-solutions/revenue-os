- 2026-09-25 · **Scheduler database-error policy** (amended 2026-10-18 by [#189](https://github.com/Peertopeer-solutions/revenue-os/pull/189)): a failed write undoes that lead's step and retries it; after 3 failed tries the lead's sequence is parked as failed for a person to look at; a broken sequence definition is parked at once; other leads keep running. That holds for steps with no outside call. A step whose send may already have reached the provider (a call or a WhatsApp message) is never undone and retried: the worker saves the send before it calls the provider (the sends table), a run of the job that finds it saved never sends again, and when the provider's answer is unknown the lead is handed to a person (a review task saying why; a call's sequence is parked as failed) instead of sending again. No sender can look a send up with its provider yet, so for now an unknown answer always goes to a person.
