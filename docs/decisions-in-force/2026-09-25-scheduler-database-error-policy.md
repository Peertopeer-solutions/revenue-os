- 2026-09-25 · **Scheduler database-error policy** (amended 2026-10-18 by [#189](https://github.com/Peertopeer-solutions/revenue-os/pull/189)): a failed write undoes that lead's step and retries it; after 3 failed tries the lead's sequence is parked as failed for a person to look at; a broken sequence definition is parked at once; other leads keep running. That holds for steps with no outside call. A call or WhatsApp job retries 3 times, about 2, 4 and 8 seconds apart, and a retry never sends twice: the guardrail check and the saving of the send share one transaction, so a failure before the provider is called sends nothing and the job simply runs again, and a run that finds the send saved never sends it again. When the provider's answer is unknown the lead is handed to a person (a review task saying why; a call's sequence is parked as failed) instead of sending again. A job that fails every try reaches its dead letter (call.post), which hands an unanswered send over the same way, says so when the provider took the send but what followed failed, and otherwise gives a person a review task, parking a call's sequence. No sender can look a send up with its provider yet, so for now an unknown answer always goes to a person.
