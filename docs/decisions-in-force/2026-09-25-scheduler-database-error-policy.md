- 2026-09-25 · **Scheduler database-error policy** (amended 2026-10-18 by [#189](https://github.com/Peertopeer-solutions/revenue-os/pull/189)): a failed write undoes that lead's step and retries it; after 3 failed tries the lead's sequence is parked as failed for a person to look at; a broken sequence definition is parked at once; other leads keep running. That holds for steps with no outside call, and for a send's job until the send is saved (the guardrail check and the saving share one transaction, so a failure before the provider is called sends nothing and the job runs again). A step whose send may already have reached the provider (a call or a WhatsApp message) is never undone and retried: a run of the job that finds the send saved never sends again, and when the provider's answer is unknown, or the job fails every try after saving it (its dead letter, call.post), the lead is handed to a person (a review task saying why; a call's sequence is parked as failed) instead of sending again. No sender can look a send up with its provider yet, so for now an unknown answer always goes to a person.
