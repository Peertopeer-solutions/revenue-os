- 2026-10-19 · **A stopping worker finishes its running jobs, and a dead worker's jobs run again within about a minute** ([#195](https://github.com/Peertopeer-solutions/revenue-os/pull/195)): on SIGTERM (a restart or deploy) or SIGINT the worker takes no new job and lets running ones finish for up to 60 seconds (STOP_WAIT_MS in services/worker/src/jobs.ts), and docker/docker-compose.yml gives it 75 seconds before Docker kills it; a job still running after 60 seconds is handed back to its queue; every queue's running jobs tell pg-boss they are alive every 15 seconds, and a job not heard from for 30 (its worker crashed or was killed) is failed and run again by the next worker, instead of waiting out its 15-minute expiry; a call or message that job had started is never sent again: the lead is handed to a person (services/worker/src/sends.ts)
